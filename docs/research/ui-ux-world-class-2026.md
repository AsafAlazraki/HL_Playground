## What we already believed

`CONFIGURATOR_PLAYBOOK.md` settled the shape: a hybrid of a linear reading spine over a document and a hub that *is* the document, six stops derived from `buildSteps(quote)`, free-roam from the first press, GOV.UK's check-answers page as the hub, and a rejection of the wizard (McLaren 9 steps, Malibu 11, Bennington's server round-trip per step) and of pure free-roam scroll (Porsche's ~9,700px, ~300 inputs, 11 groups — which does not survive 2,519 pairings and a 434-trailer shortlist). It settled the refusal grammar, which is the differentiator: **the solver records the reason at the moment of removal**, so `PartnerVerdict` / `FloorVerdict` render live in the row's second line as `Not offered — [fact about the option], [fact about the rig]`, in five never-overlapping kinds (Rule / Bound / Unchecked / Supply / Money), with at most two priced minimal fixes, `aria-disabled` never `disabled`, the reason at `--ink-soft` clearing 4.5:1, and never a hide — the band header carries the count (`422 of 434`) and names the choice that caused it. It settled motion (press 90–160ms, popovers 125–200ms, drawers 200–500ms, transform and opacity only, nothing keyboard-initiated animates, money never animates) and it settled a long reject list: 3D stages, progress bars, greying without a reason, confirmation dialogs for reversible acts, gates before the tool, server round-trips per selection, and inventing any figure the price file does not carry. `PHASE_TWO.md` then pushed harder in one direction — sticky product left, scrolling option rail right, **no step rail and no progress at all**, price bar always on screen, conflict sheet with the committed total frozen until Accept, deep-linkable state as new work because the app has no router — and set the order: rail/Admin, catalogue, configurator, conflict sheet, landing, motion pass, URL state. `DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md` and `DESIGN_CONTRACT.md` fix the vocabulary: ten rules (never a literal colour; 11px floor; uppercase is a label style; 4.5:1 measured; one accent about four times a screen; type steps are sets; negative tracking as size grows; hover/press/focus on everything with press on pointer-down; undoable means toast not dialog; a thing that cannot be done says why, where it is), a four-tier ink ramp where `--fg-quaternary` at 2.8:1 may never carry meaning, `--ink-faint` at 4.7:1 as the floor that fails to 4.26:1 over a tint, eight kind hues that are a 3px rail and never a fill behind text, four radii, `--t-fast` 120ms / `--t-med` 180ms with `--ease-settle` for arrivals, two faces only (Inter and IBM Plex Mono, tabular), and a removed-list that must not come back (masthead, 260px left rail, floating windows, per-thing chrome, glass, the display serif, a bare global key handler).

**What is already good, before any gap is listed.** The refusal object exists and is live, not stored: `src/features/constraints/trailerFitment.ts` carries `SeriesVerdict`, `PartnerVerdict`, `FloorVerdict`, and `FitmentResult` splits `selected` / `unnamed` / `rejected` / `heldBack` / `floorWarnings` — the reason and the removal are the same object, which is the thing no shipping configurator found in this study does. The app already refuses to hide: `LeftOutList.tsx` draws what the product deliberately does not hold, with the counts read from the data rather than typed, "so it reads as a judgement" rather than an omission. `plainly.ts` refuses to paraphrase a recorded reason on the grounds that "an app that improves its own recorded reasons is an app whose reasons cannot be trusted". Density is already a first-class setting (`tableReadState.ts`, `density: 'comfortable'`), level-of-detail is measured rather than tasteful (`tableLod.ts`: 8.4px is the legibility floor, two thresholds with a dead band at 0.60/0.66 for hysteresis, and the store selector returns a three-valued band so a pan that crosses nothing re-renders nothing), and the performance work is recorded with numbers (6,648 canvas elements → 210, pan p90 66.6ms → 16.8ms). Undo-as-toast is built (`Toasts.tsx`), `aria-live="polite"` appears on 14 surfaces, and search is bounded rather than infinite (`perTable: 8, total: 40, tables: 6`).
