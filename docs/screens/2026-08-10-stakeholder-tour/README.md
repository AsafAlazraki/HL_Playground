# Stakeholder tour — 2026-08-10

A full walk of the product against the seeded Northside worked example
(organisation named "Harbourview Marine" for the tour), captured at
1600×1000 @2x from a fresh clone. Two sentence rules were written through
the Business Rules builder for the tour — *When Hull Length (mtr) is at
least 6, Min HP must be at least 90* (left deliberately in conflict to
show the conflict count) and *When Standard is yes, Recommended must be
yes*. Everything else is the seed, untouched.

In the order a new user meets it:
